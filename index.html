Frequently Asked Questions

What is Netflix?

Netflix is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, anime and documentaries on thousands of internet-connected devices.

How much does Netflix cost?

Watch Netflix on your smartphone, tablet, smart TV or laptop for a monthly fee. Plans range from ₹149 to ₹649 a month. No extra costs, no contracts.

Where can I watch?

Watch anywhere, anytime on any internet-connected device with the Netflix app, or on the web at netflix.com.

How do I cancel?

Netflix is flexible. There are no annoying contracts and no commitments. Cancel online in two clicks.

Is Netflix good for kids?

The Netflix Kids experience is included in your membership, with parental controls and kids-only profiles.
